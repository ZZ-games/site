How to play

  • Get closer to 21 than the dealer without going over. Aces count 1 or 11; face cards 10.
  • Blackjack (Ace + 10-value) pays 3:2. Dealer stands on all 17s, including soft 17.
  • Double on any first two cards (one card, bet doubled). Split any pair, up to 4 hands.
  • Insurance is offered when the dealer shows an Ace and pays 2:1.
  • Keys: H hit · S stand · D double · P split · Space deal · R rebet · ? hint · Y/N insurance.
